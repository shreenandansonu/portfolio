01 // About Me

About Me

  I'm Shreenandan Sahu, a Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) at Bayer AG R&D. I hold a B.Tech in Biomedical Engineering from NIT Rourkela and an M.Tech in Clinical Engineering from IIT Madras.

  I am passionate about developing innovative healthcare technologies by integrating electronics, embedded systems, software development, signal processing, and mechanical design. My work focuses on creating practical medical devices that improve diagnosis, rehabilitation, and patient care.

  Over the years, I have designed and developed projects including bioimpedance analyzers, electronic stethoscopes, wearable rehabilitation devices, biomedical imaging applications, cloud-based patient monitoring systems, and photo-biomodulation therapy devices. I also possess a working knowledge of medical device standards including IEC 60601, IEC 62304, ISO 13485, and ISO 14971.
